What we do
Every meetup is a little different, but most include some of these.
Workshops and talks
Some events have a hands-on workshop or a short talk on a tool, language or topic, followed by time to try it out and ask questions.
Coding together and getting help
Bring whatever you are working on, a freeCodeCamp lesson, a personal project or a coding challenge, and work on it with others. Stuck? Someone is usually happy to take a look.
Show and tell
Members share projects, recent learnings or anything coding related. It is a great way to get feedback and see what others are building. Want to present? Let us know on Discord.
