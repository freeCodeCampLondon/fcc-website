AI workshop slides
Slides from our hands-on AI workshops, presented by Andy (opens in a new tab) and run with Encode Club. Each workshop focuses on running AI privately on your own machine. Work through them at your own pace, or come to the next one.
The slides are password protected. Ask for the password at one of our AI workshops or on our Discord (opens in a new tab)
The code for each deck is public on GitHub. The slides from workshop 1 are readable there too; later decks load their content only once the password is entered.
