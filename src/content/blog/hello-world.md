Every programmer's first program prints "Hello, World!", so it felt like the
right way to open our blog.

This is a space for the freeCodeCamp London community: people who come to our
meetups, people who are learning to code on their own and want company, and
experienced developers who enjoy helping others get started.

## What we will publish

- **Meetup recaps:** what we built, what we learned, links to the slides and code.
- **Tutorials:** small, practical guides, from your first JavaScript function to
  running a language model on your own laptop.
- **Learning stories:** how you got into coding, what clicked, what did not, and
  what you would tell someone starting today.
- **Community news:** new events, venues, partners and projects.

## We are looking for authors

You do not need to be an expert. A beginner explaining something they have just
understood often writes the clearest guide there is. If you came to a meetup,
finished a freeCodeCamp certification, solved a tricky kata or built something
you are proud of, we would love to read about it.

## How to submit a post

Posts are Markdown files in this website's repository, and you send them as a
pull request on GitHub. It is good practice for working on a real codebase, and
the organisers are happy to help with your first one.

1. Fork [freeCodeCampLondon/fcc-website](https://github.com/freeCodeCampLondon/fcc-website) on GitHub.
2. Copy `src/content/blog/_template.md` to a new file named after your post,
   for example `my-first-hackathon.md`.
3. Fill in the details at the top and write your post.
4. Preview it with `npm run dev`, then open a pull request.

The full steps, and what we check before publishing, are in
[CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/freeCodeCampLondon/fcc-website/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md#writing-a-blog-post).
If you get stuck, ask in our [Discord](https://discord.gg/hmVycPYAue).
